The problem

Why crypto tax numbers
go wrong.

Snapshot pricing

Pricing a trade from one candle per hour — or per day — picks a point in a range. Your trade happened at a specific second.

Missing income

Staking rewards that never get booked. Spam airdrops counted as phantom income at fantasy prices. Both skew your return — in opposite directions.

$0 cost basis

Bridge a token or receive an unlisted one and the cost basis can come through as $0 — taxing the entire sale as pure profit.

FAQ

Before you connect anything.

Is it safe to connect my exchange or wallet?

Yes. Exchange connections use read-only API keys — Glide can read your trade history but can never trade or move funds. Wallet sync only reads public addresses; you never share a private key or seed phrase.

Is the free Trial actually free?

Yes. On the $0 Trial you connect exchanges and wallets, Glide imports your connected accounts, and you read your computed numbers — gains, income, losses. You only pay when you want the tax forms.

Which exchanges and chains are supported?

Direct sync: Coinbase, Kraken, and Gemini. CSV import: Binance, KuCoin, and any other exchange via the custom CSV mapper. Wallet sync: paste an address on Solana, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, BNB Chain, Avalanche, Linea, Fantom, or Cronos.

What about Bitcoin?

BTC you hold or trade on a connected exchange (or import by CSV) is fully covered. We don't currently sync Bitcoin wallet addresses directly — we'd rather tell you that here than have you find out later.

How does Glide price my trades?

Two ways. Pricing: Glide values both legs of every swap at the exact on-chain block timestamp instead of an hourly or daily candle. Classification: staking and airdrop income is booked, spam is filtered out by default, bridged assets keep their cost basis where the data allows, and unlisted tokens are priced from the liquid side of the swap — the places crypto tax data most often goes wrong.

Can I use it for stocks and options too?

Yes — on Pro and above. US stocks and ETFs, options, and wash-sale detection, combined with your crypto in one report.

Will this hold up if I'm ever asked about my return?

Glide is built to be audit-ready, not audit-proof: every on-chain line in your report traces to its transaction hash, exchange lines trace to their source records, and pricing uses fair market value at the block timestamp. You can show exactly where every number came from. (Glide is tax software, not tax advice.)
