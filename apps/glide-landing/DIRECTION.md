# DIRECTION — Glide site 4: the conversion landing page

**Brief (user-corrected):** a landing page whose only job is converting paid traffic into paying
users. Standard landing-page anatomy — do NOT depart from what a landing page is. "Different from
current" means color scheme, look & feel, and copy only.

## Anatomy (conventional by design — zero novelty tax on comprehension)
Sticky nav w/ CTA → hero (eyebrow · H1 · subhead · primary CTA + risk-reversal microcopy · proof
visual) → platform logo strip → problem (3 pains) → how it works (3 steps + CTA) → accuracy proof
($62/block-timestamp + compounding) → feature grid (6 benefits) → pricing (5 cards, Active
highlighted, Trial de-risked) → FAQ (6 objections) → final CTA band → footer.
One repeated primary action everywhere: **"See my numbers — free"** → /register. First-person CTA,
"No credit card" microcopy, message-match headline for paid traffic.

- `--bg #FFFFFF` · `--bg-soft #F5F7FA` panels · `--ink #101828` · `--muted #475467`
- `--brand #1E40AF` deep trust blue (links, accents, highlights)
- `--cta #EA580C` orange — CTA buttons ONLY (classic CRO contrast color)
Rounded-xl cards, soft shadows, familiar SaaS grammar. Font: **Inter** only (400–800).
Real platform logos (already licensed/used on prod) for recognition: Coinbase, Kraken, Gemini,
Binance, KuCoin, Solana, Ethereum — only platforms with real integrations.
